## What the hour actually is

Mobility coaching is assisted stretching and movement practice. The professional works with the range you already have, plus a pace you can keep without treating pain. Beginners are welcome if you say so in the first message. People who already train are welcome if they accept that this is still a fitness session, not a diagnosis.

The published rate is for 60 minutes. Extra people are billed. Unlike [private yoga](/private-yoga), a second person is not included. If two of you want to stretch, the extra-person line is Rp 250.000 in Bali and Rp 275.000 in Jakarta, and the floor still has to hold both of you without a walkway fight.

A click is not a booking. A video of someone folding in half is not a session plan. A WhatsApp “can you fix my back” is a reason to stop and send you to a clinician. PersonalFitness.id owns the request, the match, the quote and the confirmation.

## What it is not

It is not physiotherapy. It is not sports massage. It is not a promise that a disc, a post-surgical protocol or radiating pain will resolve on a villa mat. If something is new, sharp, swollen or recently operated, stop and speak to an appropriate licensed professional. Come back when you want a non-clinical hour.

It is not [guided meditation](/guided-meditation) with a stretch sticker. It is not [boxing fitness](/boxing-fitness) as a warm-up. Those are separate published sessions. Mixing them in one hour is not a discount. It is two scopes fighting for sixty minutes.

We will not invent a rehab plan on the mat. We will not ask you to send clinic files in WhatsApp. We will not sell nutrition as a mobility add-on. Held services stay held.

## Space, heat and permission

Tell us the city, the area, the room and a two-hour window. Indoor tile or wood is the default. A glossy pool deck at midday is a poor stretching floor. You do not need a reformer, a yoga shala or a villa gym. You do need a level rectangle, a mat if you have one, and permission to use the space.

If you are a guest, the host or villa manager should know a professional is coming. That is how the person we send gets through the gate. Public sand, beach clubs and hotel gyms that have not given written permission are not default venues.

Heat changes the work. Midday sun on a west-facing deck is a reason to move inside. Water should already be in the room. Clothes you can sit and reach in are enough.

## How the Jakarta quote is built

Jakarta mobility starts at Rp 900.000 for 60 minutes, one person, in South and Central Jakarta. North, West, East and BSD are quoted after a timing check, with travel or a decline. Building access is the product. Lifts, visitor passes, parking and a usable indoor rectangle go on the quote before anyone travels.

If the lobby blocks outside trainers, tell us before the quote. We will not pretend a refusal is a session. If the apartment is a corridor with a coffee table, say so. We would rather move furniture on paper than invent space on the day.

There is no free intro hour. The first mobility visit is the published rate, quoted in writing. If you want a block, [programmes](/programmes) apply 5 percent off six sessions, 8 percent off nine and 12 percent off twelve, on session charges, not travel. Mix mobility with training or yoga only after you know the first hour fitted the room.

Pay only on the merchant link in the quote. Never send card numbers, account numbers or ID photos in chat. Cancellation follows the written terms: more than 24 hours can reschedule once if supply allows; inside 24 hours the session is charged; a professional no-show is not charged.
