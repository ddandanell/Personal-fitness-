## Studio, home, managed

A studio sells you travel, parking, a shared floor and a package of visits that expire. The coach’s time is split. The rate per hour of attention is often higher than it looks.

A building gym sells you access you already pay for in maintenance fees, plus a house trainer if the tower has one. Rules vary. Some buildings forbid outside coaches. Some only allow them in the unit, not in the gym. Read that before you request either.

A managed home visit sells you 60 minutes in the unit, plus the coordination of access, backup and a written quote. You do not drive. The coach does. Traffic is our problem inside the clusters we publish, and your problem if you live outside them.

Rp 950.000 is the managed in-zone unit. Extra people Rp 275.000. Ninety minutes plus 45 percent. Outer municipalities quoted with travel. That is the whole trick.

## What a Jakarta quote must show

- Tower, or at least neighbourhood and building type
- Visitor process
- Parking or drop-off
- Duration and people
- Whether the session is in the unit or the building gym
- Noise limits
- Cancellation
- Named professional or backup
- Payment link, not a personal bank chat

Missing tower information is how 06:30 sessions die. We will ask for it. Other operators should too.

## Hidden costs people forget

Parking. Overtime if the lobby holds the coach. A second person who “just joins”. A 90-minute request that was priced as 60. A travel fee for BSD that appeared after the “Jakarta rate”. A package that expires while you are in Singapore for work.

Our programmes reduce 6, 9 or 12 sessions by 5, 8 or 12 percent. They also expire on a validity window. That is not hidden if you read the quote. It is hidden if you only read the percentage on a social post.

## How to compare a studio package to us

Take the studio’s 10-session pack. Divide by 10. Add your travel time as money if you care about mornings. Add parking. Ask how many people share the coach. Then put our Rp 950.000 next to that number with one person and no commute.

If the studio still wins, take the studio. If the commute is the reason you do not train, the home visit wins even when the sticker is higher. The product is the hour you will actually do.

## Building gym versus managed home visit

If your tower already has a gym, a house trainer can look cheaper because the room is included. Count the days you actually use it. Count the days the room is full at 07:00. Count whether that trainer is allowed to enter your unit when you have a child, a guest or no interest in a public floor. A managed home visit is the opposite product: we come to the unit, we do not share the hour, and the quote names travel and access. Use the building gym when you like the room. Use us when the room is not the point.

## Recurring versus drop-in

Jakarta is a recurring city. Two or three mornings a week in the same unit is the design. Drop-in is for travel months. Price the recurring block on [programmes](/programmes). Do not buy twelve sessions for a two-week work trip.

The same professional across the block is an operations goal, not a guarantee. Backup is part of the product. A solo freelancer with dengue and no backup is a different risk profile. Ask.

## Peak traffic and why 06:30 is a price in disguise

A 06:30 start is not a premium on our card. It is a supply constraint. Fewer professionals want it. Buildings may still be opening. If we cannot staff it, we will not take the money. If another operator says yes from Depok at 05:00, ask how. Latent lateness is a cost you pay in skipped warm-ups.

Evening sessions collide with rain, office overrun and kids’ homework. They are valid. They need a window, not a minute.
