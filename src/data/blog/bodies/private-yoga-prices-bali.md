Private yoga prices in Bali move on three levers: duration, travel, and how many people are on the floor. PersonalFitness.id publishes Rp 850.000 for 60 minutes, up to two people, in Canggu and Seminyak. Everything else is a line, not a surprise.

The commercial pages are [pricing](/pricing) and [Bali private yoga](/bali/private-yoga). The [homepage](/) is the rest of the company. This article is how those three levers work so a villa group of six does not expect a pair rate.

## Duration

Sixty minutes is the default. It is long enough for a settle, a stand, a floor, and a close. Ninety minutes costs an extra 45 percent of the base, not an extra 45 percent of your patience. Forty-five minute yoga is not a published SKU. If you only have 45, say so. We may decline rather than rush.

A sunrise slot is not a different price because it is pretty. It is a different price if it sits in Uluwatu and needs a travel line. Pretty is not a billing code.

Back-to-back yoga and training in the same morning is two services. Two quotes. Two recoveries. Do not ask for a combo discount that pretends one professional is two people.

## Travel

In-zone: Canggu, Seminyak, Berawa, Pererenan, Petitenget. Travel is inside Rp 850.000.

Ubud and Sanur: Rp 150.000 per visit, not per person.

Uluwatu, Pecatu, Bingin and nearby Bukit: Rp 200.000 per visit, and a realistic arrival window. Clifftop traffic is not a vibe. It is minutes.

We do not sell “anywhere in Bali” at the Canggu rate. If your villa is in Sidemen, expect a conversation or a no.

Hotel lawns, beach clubs and temples are not default venues. They need permission and often a fee that is not our travel line.

## Group size

One or two people: published rate.

Each extra person: Rp 250.000, if the floor can take another mat.

A villa group of six mixed levels is a different coaching job. We may cap the group. We may require a larger rectangle. We will not run six private lessons in one hour. If two people are beginners and four are strong, say so. The session can still be useful. It cannot be all things.

Children in the circle change safeguarding. Parent present. Fitness scope. No unsupervised class.

## What other “Bali yoga prices” often omit

Mats for every guest. Rain fallback. Teacher language. Photo policy. Cancellation. Whether the teacher is a student picking up cash on holiday. Whether anyone is insured. Whether anyone will show up if it rains.

Our quote names service, duration, people, cluster, total, exclusions, cancellation. If another quote is a voice note with a number, it is not a competitor. It is a risk.

## Programmes

Yoga blocks use the same 6/9/12 counts and 5/8/12 percent reductions. A fortnight of sunrise pair yoga is a six-session thought. A month of three slow mornings is a twelve-session thought. Price it. Do not buy twelve because you liked one sunset class in Ubud last year.

## Worked examples, not promises

Example A. Two people, 60 minutes, Seminyak indoor. Planning estimate: Rp 850.000. No travel line. No extra-person line. Ninety minutes would add 45 percent of 850.000, which is Rp 382.500, for a session total of Rp 1.232.500 before any villa fee.

Example B. Two people, 60 minutes, Ubud. Base Rp 850.000 plus Rp 150.000 travel equals Rp 1.000.000 for that visit. If you want four mornings in Ubud, travel is four times Rp 150.000 unless you move the villa into an in-zone cluster. Geography is a cost.

Example C. Four people, 60 minutes, Canggu. Two included, two extra at Rp 250.000 equals Rp 500.000, plus Rp 850.000 base, equals Rp 1.350.000 if the floor is approved. If the deck only fits two mats, the quote is not the problem. The deck is.

These examples are the estimator’s logic. They are not a live diary. Supply, rain fallback and permission can still change the written quote. That is the point of the written quote.

## Rain, heat and the “sunrise premium”

Nobody should pay extra because Instagram likes orange light. People should pay extra when the window forces a longer route or a 90-minute block. If a teacher quotes a sunrise premium with no travel or duration change, ask what the premium buys. If the answer is “the vibe”, you are buying a caption.

Heat still cuts useful work. A 60-minute vinyasa on an unshaded noon deck is a medical incident waiting for a polite name. We will move inside or shorten. We will not charge you less for being sensible, and we will not charge you more for shade that already exists in the villa.

## Language, photos and extras that are not yoga

English is the public-site language. Many Bali teachers also speak Bahasa Indonesia. If you need a specific language, name it. If you need no photos, name it. If you need no music, name it. None of those should change the base rate. They change the brief.

What does change the rate: extra people, extra minutes, extra kilometres, extra mats we must source, a hotel permit fee. If a villa wants six branded mats delivered for one hour, that is a kit line, not a spiritual upgrade.
