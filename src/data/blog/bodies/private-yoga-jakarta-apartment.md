## Space and noise in an apartment

A quiet room with a door beats a balcony over a main road. Rugs have to move. A coffee table has to move. Two mats need clearance so nobody steps into a walkway. Indoor tile or wood is the default. A glass-walled corner with a construction site next door will eat the hour.

Tell us if neighbours share walls. Tell us if renovation is happening on your floor. Tell us if a helper will cross the room. Yoga is quieter than [boxing fitness](/jakarta/boxing-fitness), but it is not silent, and it is not [guided meditation](/jakarta/guided-meditation). Those are separate sessions.

You do not need a shala, a reformer or a full-length mirror. You do need permission to use the unit and a rectangle that can hold the people you named.

Yoga includes the second person. [Personal training](/jakarta/personal-training) does not: Jakarta extra-person fee is Rp 275.000. [Mat Pilates](/jakarta/mat-pilates) bills extra people. [Mobility coaching](/mobility-coaching) bills extra people. If you wanted two people stretching, yoga is usually the cleaner quote. If you wanted two people lifting, training is the cleaner quote. Say which hour you want.

Photographs are off by default. Many buildings and many guests do not want a content shoot. Ask early if you do.
