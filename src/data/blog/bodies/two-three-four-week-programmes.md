## What you are actually buying

You are buying a named number of private sessions in a validity window, usually the block length plus a short tail written on the quote. You are asking for the same professional where supply allows. A backup is named where available. You are buying the 24-hour reschedule rule inside the block. You are not buying an open gym, unlimited WhatsApp programming, or a meal plan.

The single-session rate depends on the selected service. Private strength training starts at Rp 1.300.000 in Bali and Jakarta; yoga and Pilates have their own published bases. The eligible pack price and participant additions are shown on the written quote. Bali travel is included across Canggu, Seminyak, Sanur, Ubud and Uluwatu. Jakarta travel rules stay area-specific. Run the [pricing](/pricing) estimator to see the starting total.

Unused sessions follow the quote. They do not become cash in the villa safe. They do not roll forever.

## Two weeks and a realistic session count

A fortnight in Canggu or Seminyak can be long enough to settle into a rhythm, but that does not mean every other day must become a prepaid appointment. Mark the mornings that remain after arrival, excursions and checkout. Compare that count with the available 3, 5 and 10-session options, and use a single session when the diary is still uncertain.

It is a poor fit if your first four days are still jet-lagged and you also booked sunrise boats. Leave space. Fitness is not the holiday. The holiday will cancel the 07:00 if you stack both.

The address still matters. If you move from Canggu to Uluwatu mid-stay, tell the coordinator before the next appointment so access and timing can be checked. Travel is included across both areas, as well as Seminyak, Sanur and Ubud. The change is an operational review, not an added guest travel fee within these five areas.

## Three weeks and a sustainable routine

Three weeks gives a remote worker more choice of routine, whether the desk is in a Bali villa or a South Jakarta apartment. Start by listing the specific windows that survive meetings, travel days and building rules. A 3, 5 or 10-session pack may fit those dates; the length of the stay alone does not select the count.

Ask for a written outline after session two, not a 12-page programme PDF before we have seen you move. The outline is a working note. It is not a clinical file.

If work calls explode in week two, use the reschedule rule. More than 24 hours, we try. Inside 24 hours, the session is charged. That rule protects the professional’s diary and your remaining count.

## Four weeks and the pack validity window

A month-long stay can make a returning routine easier, but the pack must still fit its written validity window. A 10-session option may suit a resident or longer-stay guest who has ten realistic appointments. A 3 or 5-session pack may be the better fit when the calendar is less settled.

A larger pack is the wrong choice if the guest leaves before the appointments can be used. Prepay the count you can attend. The published savings are about 5%, 10% and 20% for eligible 3, 5 and 10-session packs, but a lower unit rate does not help when sessions remain unused at the end of the validity window.

Building access must be solved once. A four-week Jakarta block without a standing visitor note is four weeks of lobby roulette.

## How to choose in one pass

1. Count the mornings you can actually keep.
2. Subtract travel days, ceremonies, and the night you already know will run late.
3. Pick the smallest block that covers those mornings.
4. Choose the service: training, yoga, Pilates, or a mix quoted separately.
5. Name each address. Bali travel is included in the five named areas, while access and instructor timing still need a check.
6. Name a professional if you care. Accept a backup.
7. Read [refunds](/refunds-rescheduling).
8. Pay securely using the payment details in your written quote, never in chat.

If you cannot finish that list, buy one session from the [homepage](/) path and decide after the hour.

## Mix of services

A block is usually one service. Yoga on Tuesday and training on Thursday is two supplies. We can quote it. We will not hide the fact that two professionals and two rates are in play. A “wellness week” bundle that mixes chef, stretch, and undefined healing is not a PersonalFitness.id SKU.

You can book boxing fitness or guided meditation alongside a strength programme. Ask the coordinator to list each session and its price on the quote.

## What a programme will not fix

A programme will not make you train if you will not clear the room. It will not replace sleep. It will not be physiotherapy. It will not hold a 05:30 slot against a building that opens at 06:00. It will not convert unused sessions into a refund because you changed your mind on day three, unless the quote says so.

## A simple arithmetic check before you pay

Write the number of mornings you can attend, then compare the relevant service’s eligible 3, 5 or 10-session quote with that many single sessions. For private strength training, three sessions at Rp 3.675.000 save Rp 225.000 against three Rp 1.300.000 singles; five at Rp 5.875.000 save Rp 625.000; ten at Rp 10.500.000 save Rp 2.500.000. Check the participant rule and validity window before payment.

If your honest count is ten, the published 10-session pack is directly comparable with ten single visits. If the honest count is four, compare four singles with a suitable smaller pack plus a single appointment. Do not buy unused sessions only because a percentage appears larger. The quote should make that comparison clear.
