Choose the block with your calendar, not with the discount. A three-night villa stay should buy a single session. A month in a Jakarta apartment can buy twelve. Paying for twelve sessions does not create a 05:30 professional in a tower we cannot enter.

## What you are actually buying

You are buying a named number of private sessions in a validity window, usually the block length plus a short tail written on the quote. You are buying the same professional where supply allows, with a backup named if not. You are buying the 24-hour reschedule rule inside the block. You are not buying an open gym, unlimited WhatsApp programming, or a meal plan.

The session rate underneath the discount is still the city rate for the service. Bali personal training is Rp 900.000. Jakarta personal training is Rp 950.000. Yoga and Pilates have their own bases. Travel and extra people still add. The percentage comes off after those lines. Run it on [pricing](/pricing) so you see the total, not a slogan.

Unused sessions follow the quote. They do not become cash in the villa safe. They do not roll forever.

## Two weeks, six sessions

This is the stay block. Useful when you are in Canggu or Seminyak for a fortnight and you want a rhythm, not a once-off photo. Six sessions in 14 days is roughly one session every other day, or a 3-plus-3 week if you prefer weekends lighter.

It is a poor fit if your first four days are still jet-lagged and you also booked sunrise boats. Leave space. Fitness is not the holiday. The holiday will cancel the 07:00 if you stack both.

Travel still matters. If you bounce from Canggu to Uluwatu mid-stay, say so. The travel fee is per visit. A block does not erase geography.

## Three weeks, nine sessions

Nine sessions in 21 days is the remote-worker shape: three sessions a week for people who have a desk in the villa. It is also the shape for a Jakarta sprint when you know you will be in South Jakarta the whole time.

Ask for a written outline after session two, not a 12-page programme PDF before we have seen you move. The outline is a working note. It is not a clinical file.

If work calls explode in week two, use the reschedule rule. More than 24 hours, we try. Inside 24 hours, the session is charged. That rule protects the professional’s diary and your remaining count.

## Four weeks, twelve sessions

Twelve sessions in 28 days is the resident default. Jakarta professionals who train before work use it. Month-long Bali stays use it when the villa does not change.

It is the wrong buy if you fly out on day 18. Do not prepay a month to feel committed. Prepay the count you can attend. The 12 percent looks attractive until you leave four sessions on the table.

Building access must be solved once. A four-week Jakarta block without a standing visitor note is four weeks of lobby roulette.

## How to choose in one pass

1. Count the mornings you can actually keep.
2. Subtract travel days, ceremonies, and the night you already know will run late.
3. Pick the smallest block that covers those mornings.
4. Choose the service: training, yoga, Pilates, or a mix quoted separately.
5. Choose the cluster. Do not mix Uluwatu and Canggu and pretend travel is free.
6. Name a professional if you care. Accept a backup.
7. Read [refunds](/refunds-rescheduling).
8. Pay on the merchant link, never in chat.

If you cannot finish that list, buy one session from the [homepage](/) path and decide after the hour.

## Mix of services

A block is usually one service. Yoga on Tuesday and training on Thursday is two supplies. We can quote it. We will not hide the fact that two professionals and two rates are in play. A “wellness week” bundle that mixes chef, stretch, and undefined healing is not a PersonalFitness.id SKU.

Swimming, boxing and meditation are by request. They can sit beside a training block. They are not automatically inside it.

## What a programme will not fix

A programme will not make you train if you will not clear the room. It will not replace sleep. It will not be physiotherapy. It will not hold a 05:30 slot against a building that opens at 06:00. It will not convert unused sessions into a refund because you changed your mind on day three, unless the quote says so.

## First message for a block

Write: service, city, area, preferred count (6, 9 or 12), typical window, headcount. That is enough. Open [programmes](/programmes) next to this article. Open the [homepage](/) if you also need to choose the service.

## A simple arithmetic check before you pay

Write the number of mornings you can keep. Multiply by the city session rate. Apply 5, 8 or 12 percent only if the count is 6, 9 or 12. If your honest morning count is four, you do not have a two-week programme. You have four drop-ins. Buy four, or buy six knowing two may be charged if you cancel late.

If your honest count is ten, you can buy nine and one drop-in, or buy twelve and risk two unused. Ten is the awkward number. Do not let a percentage pick it for you.

A Bali block does not teleport to Jakarta. Rates differ. Professionals differ. Travel rules differ. If you split a month across both, you want two quotes, or a drop-in in the second city. The [homepage](/) exists so you can see both without pretending they are one membership.
