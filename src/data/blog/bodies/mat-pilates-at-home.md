## What you need

You need enough floor for a mat plus walking space on at least two sides. A coffee table in the middle of the only rectangle will steal the session. You need a surface that is not wet and not a high-gloss deck in midday sun. You need clothes you can lie in and roll in. You need to tell us whether mats already exist.

Nice to have: a small ball, a band, a wall, a chair. None of those are mandatory. A reformer is not “nice to have”. It is a different service we do not run.

You need permission to use the room. In a Jakarta apartment that includes the building’s rules about trainers. In a Bali villa that includes the host. In a hotel suite that includes written venue permission, which most guests will not get.

You need a two-hour window, because the professional has to arrive, set up and leave. You need a one-line experience note: beginner, some classes, or regular. You do not need to be “fit enough”. That phrase is how people delay a session they would benefit from.

## What you do not need

You do not need a home gym. You do not need reformer springs. You do not need to already know the names of the exercises. You do not need a full-length mirror. You do not need incense. You do not need a matching pair of grips. You do not need to send spinal MRI files.

You do not need to book physiotherapy under a Pilates label. If you have back pain that is new, sharp, radiating, or post-surgical, stop and speak to a clinician. Mat Pilates can be a later fitness session. It is not the clinical appointment.

You do not need a group. One person is the published rate. Extra people add Rp 250.000 in Bali and Rp 275.000 in Jakarta, and they need floor. Small-group mat work fails when four people share a rug meant for one sofa.

## Mat versus reformer, said plainly

Reformer Pilates uses a machine with springs. Studios sell it because the machine is the room. Home visits almost never include a reformer unless someone already owns one, and even then we will not run a machine session we are not staffed and insured to run.

Mat Pilates uses the body, the floor and simple props. It travels. It fits a villa and an apartment. It is the format we publish. If a competitor quote is cheaper because it is a class of twelve on reformers, it is not a like-for-like comparison. ## How a private mat session runs

Arrive window, floor check, a short settle, then coached work. Cues are the product. If you wanted to follow a muted Instagram video, you would not be paying for a person in the room. Expect form stops. Expect fewer moves than a class. Expect to finish with the room usable again.

The session is 60 minutes unless you buy 90 at plus 45 percent of the base. Travel is included in Canggu–Seminyak and South/Central Jakarta. Ubud and Sanur add Rp 150.000. Uluwatu adds Rp 200.000.

Name a coach preference in WhatsApp if you have one. Confirmation waits on the diary and a written quote.

## Home setup checklist

1. Clear the rectangle the night before.
2. Put the mat down so the workplace is visible.
3. Kill the ceiling leak, the wet AC drip, the dog.
4. Tell the household the start and finish time.
5. Write building access in the WhatsApp if you are in Jakarta.
6. Do not move a dining table of twelve during the warm-up.
7. Have water. Have a towel if you want one.
8. Keep medical history out of the chat. Ask for a slower pace in one sentence instead.

## Limits

We will not call mat work a rehab protocol. We will not promise a smaller waist. We will not bring a reformer up a walk-up. We will not take card numbers in chat. We will not confirm a slot before the floor and the diary exist.

## Beginners, returning clients, and the “I did reformer once” problem

If you have never done Pilates, say beginner. If you did reformer classes for a year, say that, and then accept that mat will feel different. The machine gave you rails. The floor will not. That is not a downgrade. It is a different skill. We will not rush you into advanced shapes to prove the private rate was worth it.

If you are returning after a long gap, the first home session is a map, not a punishment. Fewer moves. More stops. Same 60 minutes.
