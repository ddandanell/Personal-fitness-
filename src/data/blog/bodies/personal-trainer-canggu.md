## What in-zone means in Canggu

In-zone means the published 60-minute personal training rate already includes travel for this cluster. It does not mean every villa is easy. Berawa compounds, Pererenan lanes and Echo Beach addresses can have very different unloading, staff rules and indoor rectangles. Name the area inside Canggu, not only “Bali”.

The hour is one person at Rp 900.000. Extra people add Rp 250.000 each. Ninety minutes add 45 percent of the base rate. Do not mix those rules in one WhatsApp sentence.

A click is not a booking. A villa host saying “we have a trainer” is not this service. PersonalFitness.id matches, quotes and confirms. There is no public trainer directory.

## Access, heat and the floor

Canggu mornings compete with breakfast, school runs, scooters and heat. Send a two-hour window, not 07:00 exactly. Indoor tile or wood with a mat beats a glossy pool deck at 13:00. If the only space is outdoors, say so. Heat and glare change the work. We would rather shorten intervals than pretend a midday terrace is a studio.

Permission matters. If you are a guest, the host or villa manager should know a professional is coming. Narrow lanes, a side gate and a place to unload belong in the first message. Dogs, children and a video call in the next chair should be named too.

Public sand and packed beach clubs are not default venues. Written venue permission, a usable private space and any venue fee would sit on the quote. We do not sell “training on Batu Bolong beach” as a product.

The Denpasar office at Jl. Tukad Barito Timur III No.16 is an office, not a Canggu studio and not a walk-in. You train at the villa you already have permission to use.
