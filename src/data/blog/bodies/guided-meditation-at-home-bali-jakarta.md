Private guided meditation at a villa or home is a quiet 60-minute sit, not a therapy session. PersonalFitness.id publishes the hour for one or two people: Rp 800.000 in Bali clusters and Rp 850.000 in Jakarta clusters. It is not counselling, not clinical treatment, and not a breathwork show. The commercial pages are [guided meditation](/guided-meditation), [meditation in Bali](/bali/guided-meditation) and [meditation in Jakarta](/jakarta/guided-meditation). This article explains what you are buying, what you are not, and how the quote is built.

If you searched for meditation at home because a group class felt public, start with the room. A chair or a mat, a door that can close, and a household that can stay quiet for an hour matter more than incense. We do not need a shrine. We do need permission to use the space and a window you can actually keep.

## What the hour is, and what it is not

The professional guides a sit matched to the brief you approved: beginners, returning practitioners, or people who want a structured quiet hour during a stay. Two people are included in the published rate when the floor allows it. A third person is not a casual extra. Larger groups need space approval and a different quote, the same way [private yoga](/private-yoga) handles extra people.

The session is fitness-adjacent stillness. It is not psychotherapy. It is not a medical assessment. It is not a promise that anxiety, insomnia or grief will resolve in 60 minutes. If you need a clinician, stop and speak to one. Do not send diagnosis letters in WhatsApp. We will not relabel treatment as meditation to take the payment.

A click is not a booking. A spa menu screenshot is not this service. PersonalFitness.id owns the request, the match, the quote and the confirmation. You do not pick from a public directory of teachers.

## Space, sound and household rules

Tell us the city, the area, indoor or shaded outdoor, and a two-hour window. Indoor is the default. A windy deck at 13:00 is a poor sit. Dogs, children, staff crossing the room, and a video call in the next chair will eat the hour. Say so if the household cannot be quiet. We would rather quote a different session, such as [private yoga](/private-yoga) or [mobility coaching](/mobility-coaching), than pretend silence exists.

Permission matters. If you are a guest, the host or villa manager should know a professional is coming. Hotel rooms and beach clubs need written venue permission and a usable private space. Public sand is not a default venue.

Two people sharing the hour should be able to sit in the same room without turning it into two private classes. If one person wants movement and the other wants silence, that is two sessions, not one clever compromise.

Sunrise captions do not change the rate. We charge duration and travel, not a photograph. Rain needs an indoor fallback. Outdoor-only decks get moved or rescheduled.

## Programmes, yoga and what you actually pay

There is no free intro sit. The first meditation visit is the published rate, quoted in writing. [Programmes](/programmes) apply 5 percent, 8 percent and 12 percent savings on six, nine and twelve session charges, not on travel. You can mix meditation with yoga or personal training in a block only after the first paid hour proved the room works.

Yoga and meditation are neighbours, not the same product. Yoga includes two people and movement. Meditation includes two people and a sit. Do not ask us to fold both into one 60-minute visit. That is two hours.

Pay only on the merchant link in the quote. Never send card numbers in chat. Cancellation follows the written terms on [refunds](/refunds-rescheduling): more than 24 hours can reschedule once if supply allows; inside 24 hours the session is charged; a professional no-show is not charged.
