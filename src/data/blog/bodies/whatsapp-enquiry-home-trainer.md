If you searched how to book a personal trainer at home, start with city, area, session, window and headcount. That is enough to check supply. Everything else can wait for the written quote.

## The five lines that actually help

1. The session: personal training, private yoga, mat Pilates, mobility coaching, boxing fitness or guided meditation. Name one. Do not send “wellness, maybe yoga or boxing”.
2. The city: Bali or Jakarta.
3. The area: Canggu, Seminyak, Ubud, Sanur, Uluwatu, South Jakarta, Central Jakarta, or another cluster we publish. “Bali” is not an area.
4. A two-hour window, not a single minute. 07:00–09:00 is usable. 07:00 sharp is a hope.
5. Headcount, and beginner or already training in one line.

Add venue type: villa, rented home, apartment, house. Add building or gate notes if they exist. Add language preference if you need Bahasa Indonesia or English.

That message lets us check whether we can honour the visit in a published cluster. It does not book the hour. Booking waits on a written quote plus verified payment. Read the eight steps on [how it works](/how-it-works) if you want the sequence in order.

## What never belongs in the first chat

Do not send card numbers, bank details, ID photos or medical files. WhatsApp is for qualification, not settlement and not clinical records. Payment happens on the merchant link in the quote. Screening, if it is ever required, uses a separate route.

Do not paste a clinic letter and ask us to build rehab. We do not diagnose, treat injury or book physiotherapy on this site. [Mobility coaching](/mobility-coaching) is stretching, not treatment. If pain is new, sharp or swollen, speak to a clinician first.

Do not send five preferred trainers from Instagram. You can name an approach, a language and a returning professional after a previous visit. Confirmation still waits on the diary.

## Worked examples you can copy

Canggu training: “Personal training, Bali, Canggu Berawa, villa, Saturday 07:00–09:00, one person, beginner returning after a long gap. Indoor living room. Staff can open the side gate.”

Jakarta yoga for two: “Private yoga, Jakarta, South Jakarta Kemang, apartment tower B, weekday 18:00–20:00, two people, mixed levels. Visitor QR at lobby. Quiet bedroom we can clear.”

Uluwatu boxing: “Boxing fitness, Bali, Uluwatu, villa, Sunday 08:00–10:00, one person, already boxing. Indoor gym room, high ceiling. Host knows you are coming.”

Ubud mobility: “Mobility coaching, Bali, Ubud, house, Tuesday 09:00–11:00, one person, stiff from sitting, not injury treatment. Level indoor room. I understand travel is extra.”

Those four messages can be quoted. A message that says “need trainer tomorrow morning somewhere in Bali, cash ok, can you do physio too” cannot.

## What happens after you send it

We check scope: a published fitness session, a usable private space, a cluster we publish, no clinical work hiding in the brief. Then we check supply: a suitable professional for that window and cost ceiling, or a second name. Then you receive the quote: total, extras, exclusions, cancellation. You pay on the link. You get confirmation: who is coming, arrival window, access note, preparation.

Nothing in that chain is automatic. [FAQ](/faq) repeats the same rules: an enquiry does not reserve the time; we do not cover the whole of Bali or Jakarta; nutrition and physiotherapy are not live services.

If we cannot honour the window, we say so. That is cheaper than a professional stuck in traffic or downstairs of a lobby that said no.

## Programmes and first visits

Do not ask for a free intro in the first message. We do not run one. The first session is the published rate. Discounts live on 6, 9 and 12 session [programmes](/programmes) after you know the hour fitted. Mixing six different session types in week one is a bad first message. Pick one hour. Add a block later.

If you are a villa manager booking for a guest, say so. Include the guest-facing contact, the entrance instruction and whether the guest will be present. Do not put personal session notes on a public reception timetable. Partner enquiries can start on [villa and hotel partnerships](/villa-hotel-partners) and still use the same five-line WhatsApp once the session is named.

If two households want two hours, send two windows. Do not hide a second address inside one message and expect one travel line. Canggu and Ubud are not the same cluster. South Jakarta and BSD are not the same cluster.
