If you compare those numbers to a Rp 180.000 reformer class, you are comparing a bus to a taxi. Both move. They are not the same ride. The commercial pages are [mat Pilates](/mat-pilates) and [pricing](/pricing). The [homepage](/) holds the other services.

## Four products, four prices

A mat group class: you travel, you share the coach with a room, you use their floor. Cheap per head. Thin on cue time.

A reformer group class: you travel, you use a machine the studio owns, you still share the coach. The machine is the marketing. The hourly attention is still split.

A studio private: you travel, you might get a reformer, you pay for exclusive coach time plus their rent. Often the highest sticker.

A home private mat session: the coach travels, you stay, the floor is yours, there is no machine unless you already own one, which we still will not treat as a reformer service.

Our rate is the last one. Extra people Rp 250.000 in Bali, Rp 275.000 in Jakarta. Travel extra outside in-zone clusters. Ninety minutes plus 45 percent of base.

## Why reformer and mat should never share a “from” price

Reformer requires a machine, a room that fits it, and a coach approved on that machine. Mat requires a rectangle and a mat. Bundling them as “Pilates from Rp X” is how websites lie. We do not offer reformer. We say so on the service page so you can leave if that is what you wanted.

If you already train reformer at a Jakarta studio, keep that studio. Add home mat as a travel tool, not as a replacement machine.

## What changes the home mat quote

- City: Jakarta is Rp 50.000 above Bali on the base.
- People: the second person is not free on mat, unlike yoga.
- Cluster: Uluwatu travel is Rp 200.000.
- Duration.
- Programme percentage.
- Props you want us to bring versus props you have. We do not haul a reformer. We may plan around a band.

A villa that wants four people on a narrow landing will get a no, not a discount.

## Studio private versus our home private

Take a studio private at Rp 1.200.000 and add your car time. Take our Rp 1.000.000 Jakarta home rate with no car time. Then ask which hour you will actually keep at 06:30. The cheaper life is the hour that happens.

If the studio has a machine you love, stay. If the studio is a 40-minute disappointment in rain, home mat is the product.

## How to read a Pilates quote

Demand: mat or reformer, group or private, duration, people, travel, cancellation, named coach, payment path. If the quote says “Pilates” only, it is unfinished.

Our 24-hour cancel rule still applies. Reformer studios often sell packs that expire. Home programmes expire too, but the window is on the quote. Read [refunds](/refunds-rescheduling).

## Worked examples

Example A. One person, 60 minutes, Canggu living room. Planning estimate: Rp 950.000. No travel. No extra person.

Example B. Two people, 60 minutes, Kebayoran apartment. Jakarta base Rp 1.000.000 plus extra person Rp 275.000 equals Rp 1.275.000, plus building access still has to work.

Example C. One person, 60 minutes, Uluwatu. Bali base Rp 950.000 plus travel Rp 200.000 equals Rp 1.150.000. If you also want 90 minutes, add 45 percent of 950.000 (Rp 427.500) before travel, because travel is per visit, not per minute.

Example D. Twelve Jakarta sessions, one person, in-zone. Rp 1.000.000 times 12 equals Rp 12.000.000, minus 12 percent equals Rp 10.560.000. That only helps if you attend. Four unused sessions at the end of a pack is how “cheap” becomes expensive.

## What a reformer studio should put on its quote, so you can walk away cleanly

If you are still shopping reformer, ask them to write: machine or mat, springs condition, class size, your commute, pack expiry, late-cancel rule, and whether a private reformer hour is even available. Then put our mat home rate next to their private reformer rate, not next to their group class.

If their private reformer is Rp 1.500.000 and you love the machine, take it. If their group class is Rp 200.000 and you never go because of traffic, our Rp 1.000.000 home hour may be the cheaper habit. The spreadsheet needs the skip rate, not only the sticker.

## Props, flooring and the false economy of “I have a carpet”

Thick carpet eats stability. Glossy tile eats wrists if it is dusty. A wet bathroom is not a studio. A rooftop with no wall is a wind problem. These do not always change the IDR figure. They change whether we accept the booking. A declined session at Rp 0 is cheaper than a session that should not have run.

If you want us to bring extra bands for a pair, say so. If you want a magic circle, say so. If you want a tower, Cadillac or chair, stop. That is studio equipment.
