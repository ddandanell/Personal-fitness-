## What “managed” means on the day

Managed means one company owns the request. You do not ping five numbers and hope someone is free at 07:00. You send the city, area, window and a one-line goal. We check that a suitable professional can take that exact scope, then we send the total. Payment happens on the merchant link in the quote. Confirmation names the person, the arrival window and the access note.

That sequence exists because villa training fails on boring details. A locked side gate, a wet deck, a dog, a helper who was not told, or a living room full of luggage will eat the hour. Coordination is the product. The squat is what happens after the gate opens.

A click on a website is not a booking. Tentative trainer interest is not supply. A screenshot of a transfer is not settlement. If those three sentences annoy you, this is still the right service. If you want a marketplace grid with green “available now” dots, we will not invent them.

## What the 60 minutes actually include

The published Bali personal training rate is Rp 900.000 for 60 minutes, one person, in-zone. In-zone here means the Canggu–Seminyak cluster, including Berawa, Pererenan and Petitenget. The hour covers a session matched to the goal you approved, a professional approved for personal training in that cluster, and travel that is already inside the rate when you are in-zone.

Simple kit on site can be used: a mat, a band, a pair of dumbbells if they exist. We do not assume a villa gym. We also do not pretend a glossy pool deck at 13:00 is a studio. Indoor tile or wood with a mat is the default. Outdoor work is possible when shade, heat and neighbours allow it. Say so in the first message.

The session is fitness. It is not physiotherapy, not a medical assessment, and not a promise that your body will change in four days of holiday. Beginners and people returning after a long gap are welcome. Write that in one line. Do not paste a clinic letter into WhatsApp.

## Space, heat and permission

Tell us the area, the floor you want to use, whether staff will be present, and a two-hour window rather than a single minute. Permission matters. If you are a guest, the host or villa manager should know a professional is coming. That is how the person we send gets through the gate.

Heat changes the work. Midday sun on a west-facing deck is a reason to shorten intervals or move inside, not a reason to perform. Water should already be in the room. Training clothes you can squat in are enough. You do not need a branded kit wall.

If children, dogs or a video call will share the space, say so. A good coach can work with a household. A surprised coach cannot rewind the first 15 minutes.

## How the quote is built

The [pricing page](/pricing) uses the same blocks operations uses. Bali personal training starts at Rp 900.000. Extra people add Rp 250.000 each. Ninety minutes add 45 percent of the base rate. Ubud and Sanur add Rp 150.000 travel per visit. Uluwatu adds Rp 200.000. Venue hire, parking and villa-staff fees stay extra if they exist.

A two-week block is six sessions at 5 percent off. Three weeks is nine sessions at 8 percent. Four weeks is twelve sessions at 12 percent. Those reductions are mechanical. They are not a mystery package. Unused sessions follow the validity window on the quote.

If you want a named professional, say so in WhatsApp. Preference is allowed. Instant assignment is not. If that person is already booked, we offer a second match for the same service and cluster.

## What we will not sell you

We will not claim island-wide coverage. We will not run a session on packed beach-club sand as a default venue. We will not treat pain, diagnose, or relabel rehab as “mobility” to take the payment. We will not take card numbers in chat. We will not confirm a time before supply and access are checked.

Public sand, temples and busy bars need written venue permission and a usable private space. Most guests do not have that. Most guests have a villa floor. That is the product.
