Extra people change the price because they change the work. PersonalFitness.id publishes pair-included rates only for private yoga and guided meditation. Personal training, mat Pilates, mobility coaching and boxing fitness bill extra people, or refuse a second person entirely. The commercial pages are [pricing](/pricing), [private yoga](/private-yoga) and [personal training](/personal-training). This article is the investigation layer: who is included, who is billed, and how not to hide a group in one WhatsApp.

If you searched whether two people can share a personal trainer at home, start with the session name. Yoga for two is not training for two. Boxing is one person. Mixing those rules in one message produces a quote nobody wanted.

## The published pair rule

[Private yoga](/private-yoga) includes up to two people in the published rate: Rp 850.000 in Bali and Rp 900.000 in Jakarta for 60 minutes, in-zone, when the floor holds both mats. [Guided meditation](/guided-meditation) uses the same pair rule: Rp 800.000 in Bali and Rp 850.000 in Jakarta. A third person is not “just another mat”. Extra people are quoted if space allows.

[Personal training](/personal-training) is priced for one person: Rp 900.000 in Bali and Rp 950.000 in Jakarta. Extra people add Rp 250.000 in Bali and Rp 275.000 in Jakarta, once per extra person, not per minute. [Mat Pilates](/mat-pilates) and [mobility coaching](/mobility-coaching) follow that extra-person line. [Boxing fitness](/boxing-fitness) does not: the published boxing rate is one person after a space check. Two boxers is two hours or a different session.

A click is not a booking. A villa of four guests is not automatically a group class. Larger groups need space approval and a written quote.

## Why yoga includes two and training does not

Yoga for two is still one teaching load when the brief is shared: one pace, two mats, one professional. Training for two is two bodies, two loads, and usually two different strengths. We can still run it. We bill it. We will not run two private classes in one hour and charge for one.

If one person wants a sweaty interval session and the other wants to stretch, do not book training and hope. Book [personal training](/personal-training) for the first and [mobility coaching](/mobility-coaching) or [private yoga](/private-yoga) for the second. Those are separate published hours.

Jakarta pair yoga is covered in [private yoga in a Jakarta apartment](/resources/private-yoga-in-a-jakarta-apartment-space-access-and-pair-pricing). Bali pair yoga is covered in [private yoga in Bali](/resources/private-yoga-in-bali-one-to-one-couples-and-villa-groups-explained).

## Space still decides, even when the rate includes two

A pair rate is not a promise that every living room holds two mats plus a walkway. If the rectangle is a corridor, we will say so before you pay. Indoor tile or wood is the default. A glossy pool deck at midday is a poor second-mat floor.

Tell us headcount in the first message. Adding four friends at 22:00 does not keep the pair price. The [WhatsApp enquiry template](/resources/what-to-put-in-a-whatsapp-enquiry-for-a-home-trainer) exists so headcount is not an afterthought.

## How the quote is built

The [pricing page](/pricing) uses the same blocks operations uses. Start with the city session rate. Add extra people if the session bills them. Add 45 percent of the base for 90 minutes. Add Ubud or Sanur travel of Rp 150.000, or Uluwatu of Rp 200.000, when those clusters apply. Canggu, Seminyak, South Jakarta and Central Jakarta do not add travel on core sessions.

Programme reductions of 5, 8 and 12 percent apply to session charges, including extra-person fees that sit on those session charges. Travel is not reduced. Read [programmes](/programmes) before you prepay a block for two people who might not both attend every hour.

There is no free extra person “because it is a couple”. There is no sunrise pair premium. There is no child-on-the-mat exception unless the quote says so, and a parent or guardian must stay for any minor.

Worked examples help. Canggu yoga for two at the published Bali rate stays Rp 850.000 when both people are in the hour and the floor holds both mats. Canggu personal training for two is Rp 900.000 plus Rp 250.000. Jakarta training for two is Rp 950.000 plus Rp 275.000. Jakarta yoga for two stays Rp 900.000. Ubud adds travel once per visit, not per person. Boxing does not get a second pad-holder on the published line; read [what pad-only means](/resources/private-boxing-fitness-at-home-what-pad-only-means) before you ask.

If you are comparing a studio couple-class to a villa pair hour, they are not like for like. The studio is a shared room. The villa hour is private, at your place, with access and cancellation written on the quote. Use [personal trainer Bali prices](/resources/personal-trainer-bali-prices-how-to-compare-quotes-like-for-like) when the comparison is training, not yoga.

## What we will not sell you

We will not hide a group class inside a private rate. We will not let boxing become two-person pad sharing on the published one-person line. We will not treat a third yoga guest as complimentary. We will not take card numbers in WhatsApp +62 811-3803-488. Pay only on the merchant link in the written quote.

If someone in the pair has pain, swelling or a recent procedure, that person needs a clinician first. We will not run rehab under a couple-yoga label.

## How to send the first message

Name the session, the city, the area, a two-hour window, and the exact headcount. Write “two people, yoga, mixed levels” or “training, one extra person” in one line. If a child will be in the room but not in the session, say that too.

## A checklist you can actually use

1. Name the session before you name the people.
2. Check whether that session includes two or bills extra people.
3. Boxing: assume one person.
4. Yoga and meditation: two included if the floor allows.
5. Training, Pilates, mobility: extra-person fee.
6. Measure the rectangle for every mat.
7. Send the headcount in the first WhatsApp.
8. Wait for the written quote. Pay only on the link.
