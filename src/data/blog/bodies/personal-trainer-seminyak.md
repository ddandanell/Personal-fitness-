## What in-zone means in Seminyak

In-zone means the published 60-minute personal training rate already includes travel for this cluster. It does not mean every lane is easy. One-way streets, villa-staff rules and parking restrictions can delay a professional even when the fee is already inside the rate. Name Seminyak or Petitenget, not only “Bali”.

The hour is one person at Rp 900.000. Extra people add Rp 250.000 each. Ninety minutes add 45 percent of the base rate. Yoga in the same villa is a different product: [private yoga in Bali](/private-yoga) includes two people in the published rate. Do not mix those rules in one WhatsApp sentence.

A click is not a booking. A villa Instagram that says “we can arrange a trainer” is not this service. PersonalFitness.id matches, quotes and confirms. There is no public trainer directory.

## Compact villas, heat and permission

Seminyak mornings compete with breakfast, drivers and heat. Send a two-hour window, not 07:00 exactly. Indoor tile or wood with a mat beats a glossy pool deck at 13:00. If the only space is a narrow deck over the street, say so. Noise and neighbours change the work.

Permission matters. If you are a guest, the host or villa manager should know a professional is coming. Side gates and a place to unload belong in the first message. Dogs, children and a video call in the next chair should be named too.

Public sand and packed beach clubs are not default venues. Written venue permission, a usable private space and any venue fee would sit on the quote. We do not sell “training on Seminyak beach” as a product.

The Denpasar office at Jl. Tukad Barito Timur III No.16 is an office, not a Seminyak studio and not a walk-in. You train at the villa you already have permission to use.

## How the quote is built

The [pricing page](/pricing) uses the same blocks operations uses. Seminyak personal training starts at Rp 900.000 for 60 minutes, one person. Canggu is the same in-zone cluster. Ubud and Sanur are not: they add Rp 150.000 travel per visit. Uluwatu adds Rp 200.000. If your villa is in Petitenget, you are still in-zone. If your villa is in Ubud, you are not.

A two-week block is six sessions at 5 percent off. Three weeks is nine at 8 percent. Four weeks is twelve at 12 percent. Those reductions apply to session charges, not travel. In Seminyak, travel is already inside the rate. Unused sessions follow the validity window on the quote. Read [two, three or four weeks](/resources/two-three-or-four-weeks-choosing-a-fitness-programme-for-your-stay) if you are staying longer than a long weekend.

There is no free intro hour. The first visit is the published rate, quoted in writing. Compare quotes like for like using [personal trainer Bali prices](/resources/personal-trainer-bali-prices-how-to-compare-quotes-like-for-like).

If the stay is actually Canggu, use [personal trainer in Canggu](/resources/personal-trainer-in-canggu-villa-access-and-in-zone-rates). Honest geography is cheaper than a wasted visit.

Seminyak traffic is not Canggu traffic. A Petitenget pin can still sit behind a one-way loop that adds fifteen minutes. That is why we ask for a two-hour window. It is also why we will not confirm a single minute before supply and access are checked. If the villa manager can send an entrance voice note, send it. A map pin without a gate is a delay.

Couples often want yoga at 07:00 and training at 08:00 for one person. Those are two hours, two quotes, or a programme once the first paid visit fitted. Do not hide both in “a wellness morning”. ## Seminyak versus Canggu on the same rate

The rate is the same. The lanes are not. Canggu often means scooters, compounds and a longer unload. Seminyak often means a tighter indoor rectangle and a one-way street. Send the pocket. Do not send “Bali, somewhere nice”. If you are between Petitenget and Oberoi, say which side of the one-way you are on. That single line saves the first ten minutes of the hour.
