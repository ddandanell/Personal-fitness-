## Why the lobby decides the hour

In Jakarta the session is often won or lost downstairs. A professional who cannot get a guest QR, a parking slot or a tower name will burn 20 minutes of a 60-minute booking. Put the building, tower, parking instruction and security process in the enquiry. If a KTP photo or a resident WhatsApp group approval is required, say that before confirmation. Do not leave it as a surprise for 06:25.

Clear a rectangle big enough for a mat plus two steps in every direction. Move the coffee table. Warn anyone on a video call in the next room. Sixty minutes means 60 minutes of work, not a tour of the unit.

Recurring programmes need a standing access note. We keep that on the booking record so the professional is not renegotiating the front desk every Tuesday. If your building changes visitor rules on public holidays, tell us.

## What the first session is for

The first home session is a working hour, not a medical exam. Write one honest line: beginner, returning after a long gap, or already training. That is enough to match a professional. Wear clothes you can squat in. Have water. If you only have bodyweight space, say so. A good coach does not need a villa gym to start, and a Jakarta living room is usually smaller than a Canggu deck.

## Building access, neighbours and noise

Pad work and boxing fitness need a room that can take impact noise. Standard personal training does not. If your downstairs neighbour has already complained about chairs, do not request jump work. Tell us the floor number and whether the unit sits above a bedroom. We will keep the session on the floor.

Helpers, children and household staff are part of Jakarta homes. They can stay. They should not walk through a loaded hinge. Give them a two-minute briefing: the session has a start and a finish. Spectators are fine. Coaching the helper is not included unless they are a paid extra person.

If you live in a serviced apartment, ask whether outside trainers are allowed. Some buildings want a vendor letter. Some want nothing. Guessing is how first sessions die in the lobby.

## How to set up the room the night before

1. Pick the room with the most continuous floor, not the room with the nicest view.
2. Remove the coffee table or slide it to a wall.
3. Put a mat down even if the coach brings another. It marks the workplace.
4. Charge the phone you will use for the access QR.
5. Write the tower, floor and unit on a note by the door.
6. Tell security the name PersonalFitness.id and the arrival window.
7. Put water in the room. Put dogs in another room if they guard strangers.
8. Decide whether the AC stays on. Heat plus humidity plus a closed unit is a different session than a villa breeze.

## Limits you should want us to keep

Home personal training in Jakarta does not replace a clinician. Pain, swelling or a recent procedure needs qualified advice first. We will not diagnose on your rug. We will not promise fat-loss numbers. We will not fight your building’s security SOP.
