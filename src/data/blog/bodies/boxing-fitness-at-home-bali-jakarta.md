## What pad-only means on the day

Pad-only means the professional holds pads and you hit them with an agreed pattern. It is fitness. It is not a bout. It is not sparring with another guest. It is not a promise that you will learn to fight. Beginners are welcome if you say so in the first message. People who already box are welcome if they accept that this hour still follows house rules: one person, no sparring, no clinical treatment.

The published rate is for 60 minutes and one participant. Extra people are not a casual add-on on this session. If two of you want to train, that is two quotes or a different session such as [personal training](/personal-training) where an extra person can be billed. Do not assume a villa group can share pads in one hour. The floor and the insurance line will not stretch that far.

A click is not a booking. A photo of gloves is not a space check. A WhatsApp “yes” from a coach you found elsewhere is not this service. PersonalFitness.id owns the request, the match, the quote and the confirmation.

## Space, noise and what we will refuse

Tell us the city, the area, the room and a two-hour window. Indoor tile or wood is the default. A glossy pool deck at midday is a bad boxing floor. Glass walls, low lamps and wet stone are reasons to move or decline. Ceiling height matters. If a punch cannot travel without hitting a fan, we will not pretend it can.

Noise is a neighbour issue, not a vibe. Pad work has a rhythm. Apartments and close villas need that named in the enquiry. If the building forbids impact noise after 21:00, say so. We would rather quote an earlier window than collect a complaint.

Permission matters. If you are a guest, the host or villa manager should know a professional is coming with pads. That is how the person we send gets through the gate. Public sand, beach clubs and hotel gyms that have not given written permission are not default venues.

We will not run kickboxing as a secret extra. We will not put two strangers in a living room to spar. We will not treat a sore shoulder as a boxing injury clinic. Pain, swelling or a recent procedure needs a clinician first. Do not send medical files in WhatsApp.
