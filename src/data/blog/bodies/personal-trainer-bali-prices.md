## The unit you should demand

Ask every quote to name:

- Service: personal training, not “wellness”
- Duration: 45, 60 or 90 minutes
- People included
- Cluster: Canggu–Seminyak in-zone, or Ubud/Sanur/Uluwatu with a travel figure
- What kit is assumed
- Cancellation window
- Whether the professional is named or “we will send someone”
- Payment route

If any of those are missing, it is a conversation, not a quote. We will not pretend otherwise when we send ours.

## What Rp 900.000 contains, and what it does not

It contains a 60-minute in-zone session for one person, professional travel inside Canggu and Seminyak, and the coordination around the hour. It does not contain extra people (Rp 250.000 each), 90-minute uplift (45 percent of base), Ubud or Sanur travel (Rp 150.000), Uluwatu travel (Rp 200.000), villa staff fees, parking, or a reformer that is not in the house.

It does not contain a free intro hour unless a separate campaign says so in writing. See the article on paid first sessions in this cluster.

Taxes, payout charges and pool entry are excluded unless stated. We will not bury them in a “from” price on a banner.

## How to line two quotes up

Put them in a table, even a note on your phone.

1. Convert both to 60 minutes. A 45-minute session that looks cheaper may be the same rate per minute.
2. Count people. A pair price against a solo price is a different product. Our yoga rate includes two. Our training rate does not.
3. Put travel on its own row. “Free anywhere in Bali” is either a lie or a loss-leader you will pay for in lateness.
4. Check cancellation. Inside 24 hours we charge the session. If another quote is “cancel anytime”, ask who funds the coach’s empty window.
5. Check payment. Card in WhatsApp is a reason to walk away, including from us if anyone ever asked. They should not.

When the rows match, pick the operator you trust to show up. Price is not the only line. It is the line people forget to define.

## Studio, hotel gym and managed home

A studio class is a different product: you travel, you share the coach, you use their floor. A hotel gym with a house trainer is a different product again, often limited to in-house guests and a menu of 45-minute slots. A managed in-villa session is the product on the [homepage](/): we travel, you stay, the quote carries travel and access.

Do not compare a Rp 350.000 group class to Rp 900.000 private training and call the private session a scam. Compare private to private, 60 to 60, one person to one person, in-zone to in-zone.

If you want the studio, go to a studio. If you want the villa hour, use [Bali personal training](/bali/personal-training).

## Programmes versus drop-in

Six sessions at 5 percent, nine at 8 percent, twelve at 12 percent. The discount applies to the session rate after extras. It is a worse deal if you cannot attend. It is a better deal if you already live in the cluster for a month. Run the estimator. Do not do the percentage in your head while drunk on a Canggu sunset.

## Red flags in Bali fitness pricing

- Island-wide coverage at one price
- Instant confirmation with no address
- “Best trainer in Bali” with no cluster
- Before-and-after fat-loss guarantees
- Reformer at home with no machine
- Pay cash to a personal account with no quote
- A rate so low the coach must stack three villas in one hour

We would rather lose the booking than match a rate we cannot honour. Public prices that operations cannot deliver are a stop condition in our own rules.

## What “all in” should mean

All in means the IDR total you will pay for that visit, including travel and extra people, excluding venue fees you owe the villa separately. It does not mean unlimited WhatsApp programming, meal plans, or a second hour if you feel inspired. It does not mean island-wide.

If you want two services in one morning, you want two quotes. Training then yoga is a nice day and a messy invoice if you pretend it is one SKU.
