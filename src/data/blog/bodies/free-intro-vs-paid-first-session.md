## What a free intro usually is

In this market a free intro is often one of four things.

A 20-minute sales consult on a mat, not a session. You leave unsweaty and enrolled.

A first class in a group that was going to run anyway. Your cost is subsidised by the room.

A loss-leader from a freelancer who needs reviews. The second hour jumps. You were not told.

A hotel amenity for in-house guests, billed into the room.

None of those are a 60-minute private session with a named professional, travel, and a cancellation rule. We will not label them as the same product.

## What you get when you pay first

You get the real unit: 60 minutes, the cluster’s travel rule, the extra-person logic, the 24-hour line. You get a professional who is being paid for the diary slot, which is how you get a backup when someone is sick. You get a quote you can compare to the second quote, because the number did not change after the handshake.

Bali personal training Rp 900.000. Jakarta Rp 950.000. Yoga Rp 850.000 Bali with two people included. Mat Pilates Rp 950.000 Bali. Those are first-session prices and tenth-session prices, before programme percentages.

If the hour is wrong, you stop. You are not trapped in a pack you bought because the intro was free.

## Why we do not teaser

A teaser hides the rate that operations must honour. Our own release rules say public prices that cannot be delivered are a stop. A free hour that converts into an undisclosed pack is the same failure in a friendlier shirt.

A teaser also wrecks supply. Professionals who work for us are not asked to donate first hours to strangers so marketing can post “free”. If we want a campaign, it will be written, dated, and sitting on the quote, not invented in chat.

## How to test us without a freebie

Buy one session. Pick a normal window, not a theatrical sunrise. Use the real room. Send the real headcount. Read [how it works](/how-it-works) so you know confirmation is the only reserved slot.

After the hour, decide: stop, buy another drop-in, or buy a block. The block is where the percentage lives. It is honest about being a volume rate, not a gift.

If you are a villa host booking for guests, do not ask us to comp the first guest so the fifth pays. Quote per session or buy a block for the stay.

## What to ask anyone offering free

- How long is the intro, in minutes?
- Is it private or a group already running?
- What is the price of the next hour, in writing?
- Who travels, and is travel in the next hour?
- What happens if I say no after the intro?
- Who is insured for that hour?

If they cannot answer, you already have the answer.

## When a paid first session is the wrong buy

If you need a clinician, do not buy a fitness hour to “see”. If you do not have permission to use the space, do not buy. If you are leaving tomorrow morning and the only window is 22:00 after dinner, do not buy. If you want a reformer, do not buy our mat session as a substitute and then complain.

## What a paid first session should contain, minute by minute

The first 10 minutes are access, floor, and a short talk about the goal you already sent. They are not a sales deck. The middle 40 minutes are the work you paid for. The last 10 minutes are a close, a room reset, and a yes/no on a next window. If a “free intro” spends 40 minutes on packages, you did not get a session. You got a pitch.

Our paid hour should feel like the tenth hour, minus the relationship. Same rate logic. Same safety limits. Same WhatsApp rules. If it does not, say so to the coordinator, not only to the coach.

## Hosts, villas and “comp the first guest”

Villa hosts sometimes want a complimentary first class to impress a arriving couple. We understand the hospitality instinct. We will still quote the hour. If the host wants to gift it, the host pays the published rate. The guest should still see a confirmation, because the guest is the person in the room. Hidden comps create hidden expectations: longer hours, extra friends, extra photos.

If you are the guest, ask whether the session is already paid and what is included. If nobody can show a quote, it is not booked.

## How to budget the test without a teaser

Pick the cheapest honest test: one person, 60 minutes, in-zone, standard service. Bali training Rp 900.000. Bali yoga Rp 850.000 for two. Bali mat Rp 950.000. Jakarta training Rp 950.000. That is the test budget. Not zero. Not a mystery pack.

If Rp 900.000 is too much to learn whether you like private work in a villa, you will not like the tenth hour either. The free intro would only have delayed that fact.
