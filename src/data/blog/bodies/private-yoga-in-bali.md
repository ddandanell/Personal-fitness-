Private yoga in Bali is a 60-minute session for one person or two people practising together in a villa or house you already control. It is not a beach class, not a retreat package, and not a public studio drop-in. PersonalFitness.id prices the pair inside the published rate because that is the teaching load we actually staff.

The commercial page is [private yoga in Bali](/bali/private-yoga). The [homepage](/) is the whole offer. This article explains one-to-one, couples and villa groups so you do not buy the wrong shape of hour.

## One person, two people, or a group

One person gets the whole cue stream. Two people share it. The published Bali private yoga rate is Rp 850.000 for 60 minutes, up to two people, in-zone. A third person is not “just another mat”. It is another pair of eyes, more floor, and an extra-person fee of Rp 250.000 if the space can take it.

Villa groups above that need a space check. Mixed levels are possible. Two private classes in one hour are not. If one of you is new and the other is not, say so. The session can still run. The cues change. What we will not do is pretend a six-person terrace is a private class at a pair price.

Children as spectators are a household decision. Children as students are only with a parent present and a session that stays in fitness scope. Unsupervised kids’ yoga is out.

## Style without theatre

Tell us slower, stronger or mixed. Hatha, vinyasa and restorative are useful labels when they describe pace, not when they become a Sanskrit lecture you did not ask for. Beginners get time. Stronger practitioners get a longer stand if that is the brief. Meditation can sit after the asana if you ask. It is a sit, not a ceremony sold as healing.

Heat and glare matter more than the playlist. Sunrise on a east-facing deck can be beautiful and still be the wrong floor if it is wet. Indoor tile with a mat is the reliable default. Outdoor work needs shade, neighbour awareness and a surface that will not cook the joint of your wrist.

If you want photographs, say so early. Many villas and many guests do not. The default is no content shoot.

## Space, mats and weather

Choose a quiet floor with room to lie down. Count mats. If you do not have them, say so. We can plan around a shortfall. We cannot invent eight mats at 06:10.

Weather in Bali is not a vibe. Rain on an open deck ends the outdoor plan. Have an indoor fallback in the same villa. If the only space is a wet outdoor kitchen, we will reschedule or move, not perform balance work on tile slime.

Permission still applies. Hotel yoga on a public lawn needs written venue permission. Most “yoga with a view” listings skip that line. We will not.

## How the quote moves

In-zone travel for Canggu and Seminyak is inside the Rp 850.000 rate. Ubud and Sanur add Rp 150.000. Uluwatu adds Rp 200.000. Ninety minutes add 45 percent of the base. Extra people beyond two add Rp 250.000 each. Jakarta private yoga starts at Rp 900.000 if you also keep a home practice there.

Price it on [pricing](/pricing). Programme discounts apply if you want 6, 9 or 12 sessions. A three-night stay usually wants one or two hours, not a four-week block.

Named teachers live on [professionals](/professionals). Lila and Arjun cover Bali yoga in different clusters and paces. Preference is a preference. The diary still has to be free.

## What private yoga is not

It is not trauma therapy. It is not clinical yoga therapy. It is not a promise of flexibility in a week. It is not a group class with a private sticker. It is not a clifftop permit we do not have.

If pain, injury or a recent procedure is in play, get clinical advice first. Do not send the clinic letter in WhatsApp. A slower session is possible. A diagnosis is not.

## How to ask for the hour

Message four facts: private yoga, Bali, area, window. Add one or two people. Add beginner or experienced. Add indoor or outdoor with a fallback. Open the [homepage](/) if you still need personal training or Pilates in the same stay.

Read [how it works](/how-it-works) if you want the eight steps. The enquiry is not a reservation. The quote is not a confirmation. The confirmation is the only document that should name the teacher and the start time.

## Checklist before you send WhatsApp

- Confirm the villa manager knows a teacher is coming.
- Measure floor for two mats plus walking space.
- Decide pace: slower, stronger, mixed.
- Name the cluster so travel is honest.
- Choose a two-hour window because traffic is real.
- Say if you want quiet, no photos, no incense, no Sanskrit.
- Keep the indoor fallback even if you dream of sunrise.
- Do not add four friends in the chat at 22:00 and expect the pair price.

## One-to-one versus two, in practice

One-to-one is for different bodies, injuries you already cleared with a clinician, or a first-ever class. Two is for couples and friends who can share a pace. If one person wants a sweaty vinyasa and the other wants to lie down, do not book one hour and hope. Book slower, or book two different hours. The pair rate is a gift when the brief is shared. It is a fight when the brief is a compromise nobody wanted.

Groups above two need a marshal. Someone in the villa has to keep late guests from joining mid-sun salutation. That someone is not the teacher.

## Timing against heat and traffic

In Canggu, 07:00 still works more often than 12:00. In Uluwatu, the route may need an earlier departure from the teacher’s side, which is why travel is a line and why windows beat minutes. Evening yoga after a full day of sun is a valid request. It is also a request for a slower class and a room that is not a deck with mosquitoes as the soundtrack, unless you like that.

If your stay is two nights, book one hour well, not three hours badly. If your stay is a month, read [programmes](/programmes).
