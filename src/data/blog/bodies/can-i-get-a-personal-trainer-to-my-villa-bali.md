## How to get your villa ready for your session

A few small things make your session run smoothly:

- **Let your host know.** Tell your villa manager or host that a fitness professional is coming, so the gate or security team expects them.
- **Pick the right spot.** A dry, sheltered floor beats a big but slippery pool deck. Indoor tile or wood with a mat works well.
- **Choose a cooler time.** Early morning or late afternoon is far more comfortable than midday sun.
- **Have water ready** and wear clothes you can squat in.
- **Share the practical details:** stairs, parking, pets, children or staff in the house. A coach can work around a busy household when they know in advance.
