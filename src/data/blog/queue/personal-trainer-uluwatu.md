## What Bukit travel means

Travel is included across Canggu, Seminyak, Sanur, Ubud and Uluwatu, including a checked Bukit address. Other areas are on request. If your villa is in Ungasan, Pecatu or Bingin, identify the exact property so access and the arrival window can be checked. If it is Canggu, see [personal trainer in Canggu](/resources/personal-trainer-in-canggu-villa-access-and-in-zone-rates).

The hour is one person at Rp 1.300.000 with travel included. A second training guest adds Rp 250.000. Ninety minutes adds 45 percent of the base when the longer appointment is accepted. Eligible 3, 5 and 10-session packs apply to the session price, without a separate Bukit travel line. Read [programmes](/programmes) before choosing a prepaid count.

A sunset reel is not supply. PersonalFitness.id matches, quotes and confirms.

## Wind, stone and permission

Clifftop decks are windy, bright and often stone. Indoor tile or wood is the default workplace. Wet outdoor stone at midday is a reason to move or decline. Boxing at the same address still needs a space check and a ceiling; this page is training.

Permission matters. If you are a guest, the host or villa manager should know a professional is coming. Hotel suites need written venue permission. Public clifftop paths and beach clubs are not default venues. Swimming stays held even if the villa has a pool.

You train at the villa you already have permission to use.

Traffic is the second product. A 07:00 request may need the professional on the road much earlier. That is why we ask for a two-hour window, not a single minute. We will not confirm a window we cannot honour.

## Bingin, Pecatu and “near the temple”

Bingin tracks and Pecatu gates are not interchangeable. “Near Uluwatu temple” is not an address. Send the villa name or the road. If the last 200 metres are stairs, the 90-minute request may be the wrong first buy: the professional still has to arrive with enough time to work, not to recover from the climb. Start at 60 minutes on a level indoor floor.

Evening sessions on the Bukit compete with traffic the other way. A 16:00–18:00 window can be more honest than 07:00 theatre. Heat on a west-facing deck at 16:00 is still heat. Move inside. The quote will not include a photographer, a cliff permit or a pool hour. Those are different products, and two of them we do not sell.
