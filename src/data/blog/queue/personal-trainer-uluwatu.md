## What Bukit travel means

Uluwatu adds Rp 200.000 travel per visit, once, not per person. Canggu and Seminyak do not. Ubud and Sanur add Rp 150.000, not Rp 200.000. If your stay is actually Ungasan or Pecatu, say so. If it is Canggu, you wanted [personal trainer in Canggu](/resources/personal-trainer-in-canggu-villa-access-and-in-zone-rates).

The hour is one person at Rp 900.000 plus travel. Extra people add Rp 250.000. Ninety minutes add 45 percent of the base. Programme discounts do not reduce travel. Read [programmes](/programmes) before you prepay twelve Bukit visits.

A click is not a booking. A sunset reel is not supply. PersonalFitness.id matches, quotes and confirms.

## Wind, stone and permission

Clifftop decks are windy, bright and often stone. Indoor tile or wood is the default workplace. Wet outdoor stone at midday is a reason to move or decline. Boxing at the same address still needs a space check and a ceiling; this page is training.

Permission matters. If you are a guest, the host or villa manager should know a professional is coming. Hotel suites need written venue permission. Public clifftop paths and beach clubs are not default venues. Swimming stays held even if the villa has a pool.

The Denpasar office is an office, not a Bukit studio. You train at the villa you already have permission to use.

Traffic is the second product. A 07:00 request may need the professional on the road much earlier. That is why we ask for a two-hour window, not a single minute. We will not confirm a window we cannot honour.
