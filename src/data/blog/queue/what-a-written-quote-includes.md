## What the quote must name

The quote names the session, the city, the cluster, the duration, the headcount, the session charge, extra-person fees if they apply, travel if it applies, programme reduction if it applies, exclusions, and cancellation. It names that payment is on the merchant link. It does not name a card number for you to type into WhatsApp.

Starting prices live on [pricing](/pricing): personal training from Rp 900.000 in Bali and Rp 950.000 in Jakarta, yoga from Rp 850.000 and Rp 900.000 with two people included, and the other four live sessions at their published lines. Outer-zone travel is Rp 150.000 for Ubud and Sanur, Rp 200.000 for Uluwatu. Extra people are Rp 250.000 in Bali and Rp 275.000 in Jakarta when the session bills them. Ninety minutes add 45 percent of the base.

If a line is not on the quote, it is not assumed. Venue hire, parking and unusual equipment appear only when written.

A click is not a booking. The eight steps on [how it works](/how-it-works) put the quote at step four, after qualification and supply, before payment.

## What the quote is not

It is not a medical record. It is not a trainer marketplace profile. It is not a promise that your body will change in four days. It is not coverage of the whole of Bali or Jakarta. It is not a free intro hour. Read [free introduction vs paid first session](/resources/free-introduction-vs-paid-first-session-what-personalfitness-id-offers).

It is not physiotherapy, nutrition or swimming. Those stay held. It is not two sessions glued into one duration. Yoga plus boxing is two quotes.

## How to check the quote in two minutes

1. Session name matches what you asked for.
2. Cluster matches Canggu, Seminyak, Ubud, Sanur, Uluwatu, South Jakarta, Central Jakarta, or a by-request area with travel.
3. Headcount matches. Yoga and meditation include two when that is the product. Training bills extras. Boxing is one. See [extra people and pair pricing](/resources/extra-people-and-pair-pricing-yoga-training-and-boxing).
4. Duration is 60 or 90.
5. Travel is present or honestly absent.
6. Programme percent is 5, 8 or 12 on session charges only, if you asked for a block.
7. Cancellation: more than 24 hours can reschedule once if supply allows; inside 24 hours the session is charged; professional no-show is not charged. Full wording on [refunds](/refunds-rescheduling).

If any of those seven lines is missing, reply before you pay. Do not send a card number to “speed it up”.

## How to ask for a quote

Send session, city, area, two-hour window and headcount. Use [what to put in a WhatsApp enquiry](/resources/what-to-put-in-a-whatsapp-enquiry-for-a-home-trainer). Run the estimator on [contact](/contact) first if you want the draft numbers in the same message.

Keep ID photos, clinic letters and bank details out. If you need a clinician, stop. We will not write treatment into a fitness quote.

## What we will not put on a quote

We will not invent island-wide coverage. We will not relabel rehab as mobility. We will not put a fake review score next to the total. We will not confirm a named professional as guaranteed before the diary exists. Preference is allowed. Instant assignment is not.

## What happens if you ignore the quote

Paying a friend of a friend in cash, then messaging us to “back it up”, is not this service. Forwarding a screenshot of our pricing page to a guest as if it were their confirmation is not this service. Changing the headcount after payment without a revised quote is how hours fail.

If the quote is wrong, reply. If the cluster is wrong, reply. If you need 90 minutes instead of 60, reply before payment. After payment, changes follow the 24-hour rule and supply. The written quote is not a mood. It is the contract for that visit, together with the service terms. Keep it. The confirmation will reference it.
