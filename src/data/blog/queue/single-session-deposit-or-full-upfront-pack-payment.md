A single private fitness session and a prepaid pack are confirmed in different ways. For one accepted session, a 50% deposit confirms the booking. For an eligible 3, 5 or 10-session pack, the full pack total is paid upfront. The written quote names the service, location, participants, dates or scheduling scope, price and payment instructions before either rule applies.

This guide answers the payment-timing question. It does not replace the [pricing page](/pricing), which owns the published rates, or the [written quote guide](/resources/what-a-written-quote-includes-before-you-pay), which explains every field to check before paying. Use it when you already understand the proposed session and want to know what payment actually confirms.

## First separate an enquiry from an accepted booking

A WhatsApp message does not hold an instructor. Sending a villa name, apartment area or preferred morning is an enquiry. A coordinator still needs to check the professional, location, session format, participant count and realistic time window.

The next useful document is the written quote. It should describe the appointment that can actually be offered. If your address, number of guests or chosen service is wrong, reply in the same thread before payment. Paying against an incorrect scope makes the booking harder to fix.

Once the quote is accurate, follow the payment instructions on that document. PersonalFitness.id does not ask for card or bank details in WhatsApp. A screenshot of the [published prices](/pricing) is not a payment instruction, and a verbal estimate is not a confirmed appointment.

## How the 50% single-session deposit works

For one accepted session, the deposit is half of the complete quoted session total. That deposit confirms the appointment described on the quote. The remaining payment follows the timing stated on the same written quote.

For example, a 60-minute private strength training single session starts at Rp 1.300.000 for one guest in a published area. If the complete written total remains Rp 1.300.000, the 50% deposit is Rp 650.000. The remaining Rp 650.000 follows the quote's payment timing. If an accepted second guest, a longer duration or another separately agreed cost changes the complete total, calculate the deposit from that quoted total rather than from the starting price.

Do not send 50% of a number you found in an old message. The current quote is the reference because it connects the payment to the professional, address, participant count and time being offered.

## Why a pack is paid in full upfront

The published [programmes](/programmes) use a counted number of eligible sessions. The current anchor packs are:

1. Three sessions at Rp 1.225.000 each, Rp 3.675.000 in total.
2. Five sessions at Rp 1.175.000 each, Rp 5.875.000 in total.
3. Ten sessions at Rp 1.050.000 each, Rp 10.500.000 in total.

Those pack totals compare with the Rp 1.300.000 single-session anchor. The reductions are about 5%, 10% and 20% respectively. A pack is paid in full upfront because the reduced per-session calculation applies to the complete counted pack, not to a series of unrelated deposits.

The quote should still make the useful operating details clear. Check the number of visits, eligible session format, participants, location, dates or scheduling process, and validity window. A ten-session payment does not create an open-ended membership or guarantee any time you ask for. It confirms the agreed pack under the written scheduling rules.

## A single visit can be the sensible first choice

You do not need to buy a pack before trying the service. A paid single visit is useful when you have only one suitable date, have not tested the room, or do not yet know whether your travel or work schedule can support repeated appointments.

Consider a short Bali stay with one free morning. Paying the 50% deposit for one correctly quoted session is clearer than paying for three visits you may not be able to attend. A Jakarta resident with a stable weekly window may find a pack more practical, but the actual appointment count and building access still need to work.

Compare the decision through the [single session and programme options](/programmes). Choose the product that matches real available dates, not only the lowest per-session figure.

## Travel and location still belong on the quote

In Bali, travel is included across Canggu, Seminyak, Sanur, Ubud and Uluwatu. Other Bali areas are by request. There is no separate guest travel fee for an accepted visit inside those five named areas. The exact property, room and professional availability still need confirmation.

In Jakarta, accepted South and Central Jakarta pockets use the published in-zone starting prices. North, West and East Jakarta remain by request, with instructor availability checked for the actual address. Use the [Bali location hub](/bali) or [Jakarta location hub](/jakarta) before comparing totals.

A pack does not erase a location change. If you move from one villa to another or change apartment towers, send the new address before carrying the remaining sessions across. The coordinator needs to check access and professional timing even when the new Bali property remains inside an included-travel area.

## Participant rules can change the payment total

The payment rule and the participant rule are separate. Private yoga and guided meditation include one or two people in their published core format. Private strength training, mat Pilates and mobility use the published participant calculation on the quote. Boxing fitness is one person only.

If somebody plans to join, name that person count before the quote is accepted. A friend arriving after payment is not automatically included. The coordinator may need to confirm the room, revise the total or decline a setup that does not fit the service.

This matters for the deposit calculation. Half of a one-person quote is not the correct deposit for a revised two-person total. Confirm the scope first, then pay against the updated written amount.

## What rescheduling does to a confirmed payment

Free rescheduling is available up to 24 hours before the session when supply allows. Inside 24 hours, the session is charged. This rule applies to confirmed single visits and scheduled pack visits.

Rescheduling is not the same as cancelling a pack validity window. A prepaid pack still follows the dates and validity terms on its quote. If your accommodation, flights or work schedule may change, read [refunds and rescheduling](/refunds-rescheduling) before paying and ask about anything that is unclear.

When a change is necessary, keep it in the same booking thread. State the session, original time and two workable alternatives. Do not assume that moving a session also moves the same professional automatically. Supply needs to be checked again.

## A practical comparison

Suppose one adult wants private strength training at an accepted Seminyak villa. The written quote for one standard 60-minute visit is Rp 1.300.000. A Rp 650.000 deposit confirms that single booking, with the remaining payment handled according to the written quote.

Now suppose the same guest has five confirmed dates and wants the eligible five-session anchor pack. The published pack is Rp 5.875.000 in total, or Rp 1.175.000 per session. The complete Rp 5.875.000 is paid upfront. Travel is included for each accepted Seminyak visit, and the quote should show the dates or scheduling scope and validity.

These are two different purchases. The pack is not five separate Rp 650.000 deposits, and the single visit is not automatically converted into a pack after the session. Ask the coordinator to issue the correct written quote for the option you want.

## Check these details before paying

Use this short review:

1. Is the service named correctly?
2. Is this a single session or an eligible 3, 5 or 10-session pack?
3. Are the city, area and actual property correct?
4. Is the participant count correct for the chosen format?
5. Are the duration and dates or scheduling scope clear?
6. Does the quote show the complete total and payment instructions?
7. Have you read the 24-hour rescheduling rule and pack validity?

For a single visit, confirm that the requested deposit equals 50% of the complete written total. For a pack, confirm that the full upfront total matches the stated session count and per-session calculation.
