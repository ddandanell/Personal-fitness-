## What beginner means on this service

Beginner means you have not practised, or you are returning after a long gap, or you have only followed videos. It is still yoga, still fitness, still 60 minutes. It is not trauma therapy. It is not clinical yoga therapy. It is not a medical assessment. If something hurts in a new, sharp or swollen way, stop and speak to a clinician first. Do not paste a clinic letter into WhatsApp.
