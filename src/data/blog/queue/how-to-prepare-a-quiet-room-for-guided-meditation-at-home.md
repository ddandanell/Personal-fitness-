You need a private place where voices can be heard, phones can stay out of reach and everyone can sit comfortably for the agreed hour. You do not need to turn a Bali villa or Jakarta apartment into a retreat. You also do not need incense, special furniture or a perfect view.

## Choose the quietest usable room, not the prettiest room

A room can look calm in a property photograph and still be difficult for a guided session. Pool pumps, road noise, housekeeping, open kitchens and people walking through the space can all interrupt spoken guidance. Start by listening to the room at the time you want to book. A villa lounge at 08:00 may be quiet while the same lounge becomes a breakfast route at 09:00. A Jakarta apartment living room may work well after the television is off and the door is closed.

The best room is usually the one you can control for the full appointment. It should have a stable floor, comfortable temperature and enough privacy for the instructor and participants. It does not need to be silent. Normal city or household sounds are expected. The useful question is whether you can reduce avoidable interruptions without asking everyone around you to disappear.

If you are staying in a villa, tell the host which room you plan to use. If you live in an apartment, confirm that the visiting instructor can enter the building and that your selected room is private. The property still needs its own access check.

## Make a place for each adult to sit

The published guided meditation format includes one or two adults. Each person needs a separate, stable place to sit. A floor cushion is optional. A dining chair can be more suitable if sitting on the floor is uncomfortable. The instructor can also sit on a chair. The session does not become less private or less useful because the room does not look like a studio.

Place chairs or cushions far enough apart that nobody needs to twist around furniture. Keep a simple line of sight to the instructor. Remove low tables, bags and loose objects from the walkway. You should be able to enter, sit and leave without stepping over another person. If two people are joining, decide that before the quote so the instructor receives the correct brief.

Do not arrange a bed as the default place for the session. Lying down may encourage sleep and can make spoken interaction less clear. If a chair or upright position is not suitable for you, say so before booking. The coordinator can check whether the request remains within the published session format. Do not send detailed medical records in a general enquiry.

## Control phones, doorbells and household traffic

A phone does not need to leave the property, but it should stop controlling the room. Put devices on silent, switch off vibration and place them outside arm's reach. If you need to remain available for an urgent call, explain that at the beginning rather than checking the screen throughout the hour.

Tell household members, villa staff or reception when the room is reserved. A short instruction such as “private session from 08:00 to 09:00, please knock only if urgent” is enough. You do not need to share personal goals or private information. If deliveries are likely, assign another adult to receive them where possible.

Pets should have a safe plan before the session begins. Some animals settle quietly; others investigate every new visitor and cushion. Use another room, a familiar resting area or a household member who can supervise. The instructor should not be expected to manage an animal while guiding two people.

For Jakarta buildings, include the correct tower, lobby and visitor procedure in the enquiry. For Bali villas, include the correct gate and the host's arrival instructions. Clear access prevents the quiet hour from becoming ten minutes of calls from the wrong entrance.

## Set temperature and light before the appointment

Comfort matters more than atmosphere. Test the air conditioning or fan before the instructor arrives. Direct cold air on one seated person can be distracting, while a closed room without ventilation may become uncomfortable. Choose a temperature that both participants can tolerate and let the instructor know if the controls are fixed by the property.

Soft daylight is usually enough. Close curtains if glare falls directly into someone's eyes. You do not need to darken the room or add candles. Open flames create a property and safety question without improving the booking. Strong incense or essential oils can also be uncomfortable for the instructor or another participant. Keep the room neutral unless everyone has agreed otherwise.

Outdoor terraces can be considered only when they are private, sheltered and genuinely quiet. Heat, insects, rain and nearby pool use make an indoor fallback important in Bali. In Jakarta, balcony noise and building rules often make the living room more predictable. The room should support the session, not serve as a photo backdrop.

## What to wear and what to bring

Wear ordinary comfortable clothing that allows you to sit without adjusting it constantly. You do not need training shoes for a seated guided meditation session. Bring water if you want it, but keep the bottle where it will not roll across the floor.

The service does not require a yoga mat, branded props, headphones or a speaker system. If you already use a supportive cushion, you can prepare it. If you prefer a chair, choose one with a stable seat and feet on the floor. A soft sofa can work, but an upright chair often makes it easier to remain attentive.

Tell the coordinator whether one or two adults will join and whether chairs or floor cushions are planned. That is useful operational information. You do not need to buy equipment before the quote.

## The five-minute check before the instructor arrives

Use this short check:

1. The instructor can enter the correct gate, tower or lobby.
2. The room is private for the whole appointment.
3. Each adult has a stable chair or cushion.
4. Phones, doorbells and avoidable interruptions are controlled.
5. Temperature, light and noise are comfortable enough to begin.

You do not need to stage the room. Leave enough time to greet the instructor and confirm who is participating. Photographs are not part of the session unless they have been discussed in advance.
