Villa managers and hotel hosts can book private fitness for guests without turning the reception desk into a clinic or a marketplace. PersonalFitness.id coordinates personal training, private yoga, mat Pilates, mobility, boxing fitness and guided meditation at properties in published Bali and Jakarta clusters. The commercial pages are [villa and hotel partnerships](/villa-hotel-partners) and [contact](/contact). This article is the investigation layer: what the host sends, what the guest never puts on a public timetable, and how the quote is built.

If you searched how to arrange a personal trainer for villa guests, start with permission and a named session. “Wellness on request” is not a booking. A guest Instagram story is not a briefing.

## What the property is buying

The property is buying a coordinated private hour at a villa, home or other suitable lawful space the guest already has permission to use. Starting prices sit on [pricing](/pricing). City clusters sit on [Bali](/bali) and [Jakarta](/jakarta). The eight steps sit on [how it works](/how-it-works).

The product is the sequence: enquiry, match, quote, payment, confirmation, delivery, aftercare. It is not a public trainer directory in your lobby. It is not a walk-in class at the Denpasar office.

A click is not a booking. A WhatsApp “we have a guest who wants yoga tomorrow” without an area, window and headcount is not supply.

If the guest wants yoga for two, that pair rule is published. If they want training for two, extra people are billed. Boxing is one person. Read [extra people and pair pricing](/resources/extra-people-and-pair-pricing-yoga-training-and-boxing) before you promise a complimentary second mat.

## Guest privacy versus access notes

Access notes belong on the booking record: entrance, parking, staff name, dog, stairs. Personal session notes do not belong on a reception whiteboard. Do not print a guest’s training goal on a public timetable. The coordinator can keep a practical, non-clinical brief with the guest’s permission. Clinical records stay with a clinician. We do not book physiotherapy or nutrition on this site.

Photographs are off by default. Many guests and many properties do not want a content shoot. Ask before anyone films.

## Recurring guest calendars

A property can enquire about a repeating guest menu: sunrise yoga two mornings a week, training for one person, meditation once. That still needs supply. Prepaying a [programme](/programmes) is 6, 9 or 12 named visits with a validity window, not an open membership for whoever checks in.

In-zone Canggu and Seminyak include travel on core sessions. Ubud and Sanur add Rp 150.000. Uluwatu adds Rp 200.000. Jakarta buildings need lobby rules on the quote. We do not claim island-wide or city-wide coverage.

The first guest visit is still a paid hour. There is no free intro for a “VIP arrival”. The host can pay the published rate. We will not hide a complimentary hour.

## What we will not do for a property

We will not staff a public beach club as a default venue. We will not relabel massage or spa as a live catalogue item. We will not send a professional through a gate nobody mentioned. We will not confirm 07:00 for a guest who has not approved the hour. We will not take settlement in chat.

Swimming stays held even if the villa has a pool. Clinical requests stay held.

## How confirmation looks

The quote names the session, people, travel, extras and cancellation. After payment verification, confirmation names the professional, arrival window, access note and preparation list. That is the booking. Put that confirmation with the person who opens the gate.

If the guest checks out early, unused prepaid visits follow the validity window. Inside 24 hours the session is charged unless the quote says otherwise. Professional no-show is not charged. Details sit on [refunds and rescheduling](/refunds-rescheduling).

## A checklist a host can actually use

1. Name the session. Do not write “wellness”.
2. Name the cluster and the entrance.
3. Name who pays.
4. Name a two-hour window.
5. Keep medical and ID files out.
6. Tell the gate staff.
7. Open [villa and hotel partnerships](/villa-hotel-partners) if you want a repeating menu.
8. Send WhatsApp. Wait for the quote. Pay only on the link.

A guest hour is useful when the property, the guest and the quote match. PersonalFitness.id exists to hold that line. If that is what you wanted when you typed the query, start on [partnerships](/villa-hotel-partners) or the [homepage](/) and send the cluster.

## Who speaks to WhatsApp

One property contact is better than five guest phones. The coordinator needs a person who can answer access questions the same day. Guests can still receive the confirmation. They should not be asked to forward clinic letters or card numbers. If the guest is paying, the merchant link goes to the guest. If the villa is paying, the merchant link goes to the villa. Do not mix those in one chat thread.

A repeating Monday yoga slot is still subject to supply. A wedding week is not an automatic surcharge; it is a diary problem. Send the dates. We will say yes, offer a second window, or decline. That answer belongs in writing, next to the rate, before anyone promises the bride a 06:30 private class on the lawn.

## After the stay

Do not keep a guest WhatsApp thread alive as a villa account. Close it. If the same villa wants a Monday slot next season, open a new thread with the property name, not last year’s guest. Rates are published and can move; do not screenshot last July.

If a guest asks for nutrition or physiotherapy, decline in the same sentence you confirm yoga. Held work is held. A manager who promises “the trainer can also look at a shoulder” creates a complaint we cannot fulfil. Keep the six live hours. Keep the written quote. Keep one coordinator. That is the whole product.
