## What outer-zone means in Ubud

Outer-zone means the published Bali personal training rate does not already include travel. Ubud and Sanur add Rp 150.000 per visit, once, not per person. Canggu and Seminyak do not add that line. If your stay is actually Pererenan, you wanted [personal trainer in Canggu](/resources/personal-trainer-in-canggu-villa-access-and-in-zone-rates), not this page.

The hour is one person at Rp 900.000 plus travel. Extra people add Rp 250.000 each. Ninety minutes add 45 percent of the base rate. Travel is not discounted on [programmes](/programmes). Session charges can be.

A click is not a booking. A jungle path is not a studio. PersonalFitness.id matches, quotes and confirms. There is no public trainer directory.

## Access, floor and permission

An Ubud property may have a peaceful deck and a complicated approach. Tell the coordinator whether the proposed area is level, covered and private. A valley view is optional. A workable floor and a realistic arrival window are essential.

Indoor tile or wood with a mat beats a damp outdoor deck after rain. Heat still exists in Ubud. Midday sun on a west-facing deck is a reason to move inside. Water should already be in the room.

Permission matters. If you are a guest, the host or villa manager should know a professional is coming. Family compounds and guesthouse suites need owner permission. Public temples, rice-field paths and hotel decks without written permission are not default venues.

The Denpasar office at Jl. Tukad Barito Timur III No.16 is an office, not an Ubud studio. You train at the house you already have permission to use.
