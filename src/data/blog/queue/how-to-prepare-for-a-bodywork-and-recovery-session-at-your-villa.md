## Start with the purpose of the hour

A bodywork and recovery session at your villa should begin with a simple question: what kind of hour are you trying to arrange? PersonalFitness.id offers a 60-minute wellness session built around assisted stretching, light myofascial release and time to relax. It is designed for one adult in a suitable private room. A second adult can be added to the written quote. This is not a diagnosis, physiotherapy appointment, injury treatment or rehabilitation service.

That distinction helps you prepare well. You do not need to assemble a medical file or choose a technique from a long menu. You do need to explain the practical outcome you prefer in ordinary language. You might want a quieter session after a long flight, a general stretch after several active days, or an unhurried wellness hour at the villa. The instructor can then keep the session within the agreed non-clinical scope.

If your main goal is coached range of motion and movement practice, compare [mobility coaching](/mobility-coaching) before you book. If you want the published assisted-stretching and relaxation format, use the [bodywork and recovery service page](/bodywork-recovery) as the commercial source. This guide explains how to prepare the property and the request. It does not replace the service page or your written quote.

## Choose a private, level room

The best room is not necessarily the most photogenic part of the villa. Choose a level, dry floor where one mat can lie flat and the instructor can move around it without stepping into a walkway. A living room often works better than a narrow pool deck. A shaded terrace can also work when it is dry, private and protected from strong wind or direct midday heat.

Move coffee tables, loose rugs, luggage, low lamps and decorative objects before the appointment. Keep at least one clear route from the entrance to the session area. If the instructor needs to carry a mat or small portable item, stairs, steep garden paths and distant parking should be mentioned in advance. The aim is not to make the villa look like a clinic. It is to make one calm wellness hour practical.

Privacy matters because the session involves guided movement and appropriate hands-on assistance. Ask other guests and villa staff not to walk through the room during the booked hour. Put pets in another secure area. If children are at the property, arrange adult supervision away from the session space. A booked adult wellness session does not include childcare.

If your villa has a shared gym, treatment room or yoga pavilion, ask management whether an outside professional may use it. Do not assume that a room shown in the villa listing is available at your chosen time. The [service-area directory](/service-areas) can help you find the relevant location page, but property permission still needs to be checked at the actual address.

## Set the temperature, noise and light

Comfort is easier to manage before the instructor arrives. In an air-conditioned room, choose a moderate temperature rather than making the room very cold. In an open room, use fans only when they do not blow directly across the mat or make conversation difficult. Keep drinking water nearby and allow a little time after the session before rushing into another booking or transfer.

Reduce avoidable noise. Silence phones, pause loud music and tell the villa team that the room is in use. You do not need complete silence, but a space beside an active kitchen, television or pool gathering will make quiet instructions harder to follow. Natural light is useful, while harsh glare on the floor can be distracting. Close a blind or change rooms if the afternoon sun makes the proposed area uncomfortable.

No candles, incense, oils or spa decorations are required. Strong fragrances can be unwelcome, and they are not part of the published session. A clean mat, a folded towel and ordinary water are sufficient unless the written quote says something else. Do not buy equipment before confirmation.

## Wear normal movement clothes

Wear clothing that lets you sit, lie down and move without needing constant adjustment. A T-shirt or training top with shorts, leggings or light exercise trousers is suitable. The client remains appropriately clothed throughout the session. Avoid clothing with hard belts, large buckles or bulky items in the pockets. Remove a watch or jewellery only if it feels uncomfortable during movement, and keep valuables in your own secure place.

The instructor should explain each stage and use consent-based professional boundaries. You can ask what a movement involves before it begins. You can also ask to reduce pressure, change position or stop an activity. Consent is not a one-time formality at the start. It continues throughout the hour.

If you do not want hands-on guidance, say so before the booking is confirmed. The coordinator can check whether the requested format is still suitable. If you prefer an entirely coach-led movement session without bodywork, [mobility coaching](/mobility-coaching) may be the clearer service to consider.

## Share useful information without oversharing

Your first message only needs practical booking information:

- city and area
- villa or home type
- preferred date and a realistic time window
- number of adults
- the general kind of session you want
- any stairs, gate rules or management permission
- your preferred instruction language

Do not send passport images, card details, bank details or a detailed health history in a general WhatsApp message. Payment instructions belong on the written quote. If a relevant limitation affects whether ordinary stretching is appropriate, state the practical concern briefly and ask whether the request is within scope. The coordinator can explain the next step without pretending to make a clinical assessment.

If you have sudden pain, a recent injury, a post-operative restriction or a condition requiring diagnosis or treatment, this wellness session may not be appropriate. Seek an appropriately qualified clinical professional. PersonalFitness.id does not turn a general bodywork booking into physiotherapy or medical care.

## Give the instructor a workable arrival plan

Send the correct map pin and a short written instruction. Include the villa name only if it is genuinely used at the entrance. If the road has multiple gates, say which one works. Tell reception or security that a wellness professional is expected, but do not put private session details on a public arrival board.

For a Bali villa, your host may know a more accurate entrance than the map pin from the booking platform. For a Jakarta apartment, confirm the tower, lobby and visitor procedure. If management needs an identification list or advance registration, arrange it before the appointment rather than while the instructor waits downstairs.

Keep 10 to 15 minutes free before the booked time. That gives you time to clear the room, change clothes and put your phone away. The session itself remains the agreed 60 minutes. Do not schedule it immediately before checkout, an airport transfer or another activity that cannot move.

Use the [Bali hub](/bali) or [Jakarta hub](/jakarta) to check how location planning changes between the two cities. The city page should lead you to the relevant area page without creating a second owner for the bodywork service itself.

## Prepare differently when two adults join

A second guest changes the quote and the rhythm of the hour. Confirm whether both adults will participate within the same 60-minute booking. Prepare enough space for the instructor to work safely while one person is moving and the other is waiting. If both guests want full individual hours, request two sessions rather than assuming the same hour doubles in length.

Tell the coordinator if one guest wants a different pace. Each adult should be comfortable with the agreed format and give their own consent. One person should not book hands-on wellness work as a surprise for another adult without first checking that they want it.

For larger groups, ask what can genuinely be delivered. Do not multiply the two-person format into a group package without a confirmed service design, participant limit, professional ratio and total.

## What should happen at the start

When the instructor arrives, show the agreed room and allow a brief setup check. Confirm who is participating, the planned finish time and whether any practical preference has changed. The instructor should explain the general structure before beginning. A normal hour can include an introduction, guided stretching or light release work, position changes and a calm finish, but the exact sequence follows the confirmed professional scope and the client’s ongoing consent.

Stop and ask a question if an instruction is unclear. Ordinary stretching can feel like effort, but the session is not a test of how much discomfort you can tolerate. The purpose is a well-managed wellness hour, not a promised result or a treatment claim.

At the end, leave a few minutes to sit up, drink water and hear any simple non-clinical suggestions within the instructor’s scope. If you want another visit, request the next date through the normal booking process. Do not assume the same person or time is held until the follow-up quote is confirmed.

## A final five-minute checklist

Before the appointment, check that:

1. the correct address, date and arrival window are on the quote;
2. the named adult participants match the booking;
3. the room is private, level, dry and clear;
4. the property permits the visit;
5. phones, pets and household traffic are managed;
6. you are wearing comfortable movement clothing;
7. you understand that this is wellness instruction, not clinical treatment;
8. the deposit or full pack payment has been handled only through the written instructions.
