Research checked 11 November 2026. These are planning recommendations for Bali, not supplier quotes or proven willingness to pay. The existing repository's published core rates and programme rules were preserved. New formats display an indicative guide or request an individual quote. Jakarta needs a separate local cost and supply check.

Premium value must be visible: a suitable professional, clear language match, realistic arrival planning, clean appropriate equipment, privacy, dependable support and a coherent next step. Avoid promising the best or largest network without comparative evidence.

## Contribution and the price floor

Calculate contribution before fixed overhead and tax. Include the professional, travel, kit/cleaning, variable coordination, payment fee, expected service recovery and any property referral commission. Do not call gross customer receipts profit.

If collected price is P, non-percentage variable cost is C, payment and recovery percentage is f, referral percentage is r and target contribution is m, a zero-tax-extraction planning floor is:

`P_min = C / (1 - f - r - m)`

Example, using assumptions rather than contracted costs: professional IDR 400,000 + travel 75,000 + kit 25,000 + variable coordination 50,000 = C of IDR 550,000. At P of IDR 900,000, with payment 3% and recovery 2%, contribution is IDR 305,000 or 33.89%. Add a 10% villa referral commission and it falls to IDR 215,000 or 23.89%. For a 30% target, the floor is about IDR 846,154 direct, or IDR 1,000,000 with that referral fee.

If tax is extracted from P, determine the actual treatment with the accountant. For the specific model where net revenue is P / (1+t), fees and commission are charged on P, and the target m is measured on net revenue, the gross price floor is:

`P_min = C / (((1-m)/(1+t)) - f - r)`

This formula is not a claim about the applicable Indonesian tax rate or treatment. Only use it when the actual charging and fee bases match. A zero or negative denominator is an invalid quote.

## Packages, travel and coordination

Use the current two, three and four week programme rules for eligible services, rather than introducing a second incompatible package price. The calculator now keeps travel outside the programme reduction. Referral fees and discounts still require a cost check. For example, a direct 5% reduction of the IDR 900,000 assumed session leaves roughly 30.67% contribution with C of IDR 550,000 and 5% percentage costs; a 10% referral on the same sale reduces that materially.

Ordinary booking coordination is already part of a normal visit. Only quote the optional IDR 1,500,000 weekly concierge planning guide for a separately scoped, more involved multi-service arrangement, with up to eight planned bookings, stated support hours and session charges separate. Do not automatically add it to every package.

Chef and equipment formats remain individually quoted. Use the existing myCHEF operation's current itemised offer [S12], not old remembered chef rates or an automatic duplicate surcharge. Ice bath and sauna rental must name the rental period, delivery, installation, care and collection [S13].

## Commercial test

Start with the 20 highlighted offer formats and their shared service families. Get actual provider costs and local capacity before accepting payment. For each of the first three priority families, record at least ten qualified enquiries and every quoted price, acceptance, fulfilment, contribution and repeat request. These are test targets, not claims of statistical certainty. Change the public guide version deliberately, honour accepted quotes and use the results to decide where the premium is supported.
